| **MotionBert** | 23.7 | 22.0 | 10.3 |

### Velocity (VW-SI-MPJVE, cm/frame) -- Optimized

| | Mean | Median | Std |
|---|---|---|---|
| MediaPipe | 3.8 | 3.4 | 2.0 |
| **MotionBert** | **2.0** | **1.7** | **1.5** |

### Win/Loss by VW-SI-MPJPE

- MediaPipe wins 14/25 examples
- MotionBert wins 11/25 examples

### Interpretation

- **MediaPipe is better on average** for position accuracy (lower mean VW-SI-MPJPE and SI-MPJPE)
- **MotionBert is better for temporal smoothness** (lower velocity error)
- **MotionBert has higher variance** (std=11.0 vs 3.5) -- it has some very good examples but also some very bad ones
- **MotionBert's median is actually lower** (9.4 vs 9.9 cm) -- a few high-error outliers drag its mean up
- MediaPipe is more consistent across examples
