## Joint Mapping Decision

After reviewing all four joint representations (MotionBert/MPII, MediaPipe, CMU GT/COCO19, and our optimizer), here is the final plan for the optimizer's skeleton.

Three categories of joints:

### Category 1: Evaluation Joints (12)

Directly detected by all three systems. 

**USED IN**: Optimization, Evaluation, Visualization

| # | Joint | MotionBert (MPII) | MediaPipe | CMU GT (COCO19) |
|---|-------|-------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | R Hip | MPII[2] | MP[24] | COCO[12] |
| 2 | R Knee | MPII[1] | MP[26] | COCO[13] |
| 3 | R Ankle | MPII[0] | MP[28] | COCO[14] |
| 4 | L Hip | MPII[3] | MP[23] | COCO[6] |
| 5 | L Knee | MPII[4] | MP[25] | COCO[7] |
| 6 | L Ankle | MPII[5] | MP[27] | COCO[8] |
| 7 | R Shoulder | MPII[12] | MP[12] | COCO[9] |
| 8 | R Elbow | MPII[11] | MP[14] | COCO[10] |
| 9 | R Wrist | MPII[10] | MP[16] | COCO[11] |
| 10 | L Shoulder | MPII[13] | MP[11] | COCO[3] |
| 11 | L Elbow | MPII[14] | MP[13] | COCO[4] |
| 12 | L Wrist | MPII[15] | MP[15] | COCO[5] |

### Category 2: Synthesized Joints (2)

Direct in MotionBert + CMU GT, synthesized for MediaPipe. 

**USED IN**: Optimization, Evaluation, Visualization

| # | Joint | MotionBert (MPII) | MediaPipe | CMU GT (COCO19) |
|---|-------|-------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 13 | Pelvis | MPII[6] direct | midpoint(LHip, RHip) | COCO[2] direct |
| 14 | Base of Neck | MPII[8] direct | midpoint(LShoulder, RShoulder) | COCO[0] direct |

### Category 3: Optimization-Only Joints (mode-specific)

Used during optimization (provides signal to FK chain). 

**USED IN**: Optimization, Visualization

**MotionBert mode:**

| Joint | Source | Why excluded from eval |
|-------|--------|----------------------|
| Thorax | MPII[7] direct | No direct COCO19 equivalent |
| Head Top | MPII[9] direct | COCO19 has Nose, not Head Top (~10-15cm apart) |

**MediaPipe mode:**

| Joint | Source | Why excluded from eval |
|-------|--------|----------------------|
| Nose | MP[0] direct | MotionBert has Head Top, not Nose |

### Key Principles

- **No inferred/computed midpoints in the optimizer's skeleton.** Previous implementation computed Hip, Spine, Thorax, and Neck as midpoints — we are removing that.
- **Evaluation is strictly apples-to-apples.** Only the 14 joints (12 direct + 2 synthesized) are evaluated against GT.
- **All available signal is still used during optimization.** Category 3 joints help constrain the FK chain even though they aren't evaluated.
