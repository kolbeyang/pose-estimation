## Phase 2: Motionbert to Camera Space
This takes place in `pose-optimizer/run_motionbert/detect.py:447` in a function called `motionbert_to_camera_space`.

This happens in a two step process.
1. Heuristic-driven scale estimation
2. Heuristic-drive depth estimation

Mediapipe on the other hand has the first step (scale estimation) built in to the model because it already outputs in world units (cm). 

I would like the approach to step 2 to be unified here as well. Just like MediaPipe, `motionbert_to_camera_space` should use `solvePnp` to essentially solve for the scale.

I am even willing to accept a slight degradation in performance here for the sake of consistency but I cannot foresee any reason why this would fundamentally reduce accuracy.
