# Plan: Joint Alignment -- Neck/Spine Remapping

**Date**: 2026-04-11
**SPEC**: `agent-team/specs/joint-alignment.md`

---

## Overview

The SPEC redefines what the "Neck" joint means in our 16-joint skeleton. The key conceptual change:

- **Old**: Joint 8 ("Neck") = anatomical neck (above shoulders). Joint 7 ("Spine") = thorax/shoulder level (midpoint Pelvis-to-Neck for GT/MP, MPII[7] Thorax for SH/MB).
- **New**: Joint 14 in the SPEC's numbering ("Neck") = thorax/shoulder level. The anatomical neck (SH[8] UpperNeck, MB joint 8) is **dropped** from the pipeline because MediaPipe has no equivalent.

The SPEC renumbers joints 1-15 (1-indexed), but our skeleton is 0-indexed with 16 joints. Translating the SPEC's table into our 0-indexed skeleton order, the SPEC asks for:

| SPEC # | Joint Name | Our Index | Source Mapping |
|--------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| 13 | Pelvis | 0 | SH[6], MB[6], midpoint(hips) for MP, COCO[2] |
| 14 | **Neck** | ? | SH[7] Thorax, MB joint 7 "Spine", midpoint(shoulders) for MP, COCO[0] |

**What changes**: The data that currently maps to joint 8 (anatomical neck from SH[8]/UpperNeck) must be replaced. Joint 8's position should instead come from what currently feeds joint 7 (SH[7] Thorax / MB "Spine" / midpoint(shoulders) / COCO[0] Neck). The old anatomical neck data is dropped entirely.

**What stays the same**: All 12 Category 1 evaluation joints (hips, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows, wrists). Joint 0 (Pelvis). Joint 9 (Nose). The skeleton stays at 16 joints. FK hierarchy structure. Bone connectivity.

**Final recommendation**: The cleanest approach is:

1. **Joint 8 ("Neck")** mapping changes:

   - MotionBERT: MB joint 7 "Spine" instead of MB joint 8 "Neck"

2. **Joint 7 ("Spine")** remains FK-internal, synthesized as midpoint(Pelvis, Neck) for all sources. Currently for MPII/MB it's SH[7] Thorax directly, but since that data is moving to joint 8, joint 7 should be synthesized as midpoint(Pelvis, joint 8) across all sources.

This only affects `mpii_to_skeleton()` which already has access to all 16 MPII joints.

### Decision 2: What happens to the old Neck (SH[8] UpperNeck) data?

It is **truly dropped** from the skeleton joint positions. However, it is still **used internally** for Nose synthesis (`nose = SH[8] + 0.3 * (SH[9] - SH[8])`). The Nose synthesis formula in `mpii_to_skeleton()` must continue to use `keypoints_mpii[8]` (UpperNeck) and `keypoints_mpii[9]` (HeadTop) as raw inputs.

The MPII heatmap channel 8 (UpperNeck) will no longer be scored against any skeleton joint. This is a minor scoring signal loss but is correct since we can't evaluate it.

## Risk Assessment
